PENGALAMAN TERBAIK DI BALI

Mengapa Memilih Paket ATV & Ayung River Rafting?

Gabungkan dua aktivitas petualangan populer di Bali dalam satu hari: jelajahi jalur off-road dengan ATV pada pagi hari, lalu nikmati Ayung River Rafting di tengah lanskap hutan tropis pada siang hari.

Petualangan ATV Off-Road

Lewati jalur berlumpur, perkebunan lokal, persawahan, dan terowongan gua bersama instruktur.

Keseruan Ayung River Rafting

Jelajahi aliran sungai, jeram, ngarai hutan hujan, ukiran batu, dan pemandangan air terjun bersama pemandu rafting.

Dua Aktivitas dalam Satu Hari

Mulai dengan ATV pada pagi hari, lalu lanjutkan rafting pada siang hari, termasuk waktu untuk bersih-bersih dan makan siang.

MORNING SESSION

  1. 01

    08:00 AM

    Hotel Pick-Up

    Private local driver picks you up from your hotel lobby for the drive to the adventure basecamp in the Ubud area.

  2. 02

    09:30 AM

    Arrival, Welcome & Briefing

    Welcome drink, registration, helmet and rubber boots fitting, safety briefing, and trial session with the instructor.

  3. 03

    10:00 AM–12:00 PM

    ATV Quad Bike Adventure

    Ride through muddy paths, local plantations, scenic rice fields, and an authentic cave tunnel. Estimated ride duration: 1.5–2 hours.

  4. 04

    12:00 PM–12:30 PM

    Refresh & Transition

    Return to basecamp, wash up, use changing rooms and shower facilities, and prepare for the transfer to rafting.

AFTERNOON SESSION

  1. 05

    12:30 PM–01:15 PM

    Arrival & River Lunch

    Arrive at the rafting start point or restaurant area and enjoy an Indonesian buffet lunch overlooking the river valley.

  2. 06

    01:15 PM–01:30 PM

    Gear Up & Safety Briefing

    Meet your river guide, get fitted with a life jacket and paddle, and receive the safety briefing.

  3. 07

    01:30 PM–03:30 PM

    Ayung River White-Water Rafting Expedition

    Spend approximately two hours on the river, navigating Class II–III rapids and enjoying rainforest canyons, stone carvings, and waterfall scenery with a guide.

  4. 08

    03:30 PM–04:00 PM

    Finish, Shower & Refresh

    Reach the finish point, shower, change into dry clothes, and get ready for the return journey.

  5. 09

    After Rafting

    Complimentary Stop: Coffee Plantation or Rice Terrace

    Enjoy a free stop at either a local coffee plantation or a scenic rice terrace before returning to your hotel. Your preferred stop can be discussed with your driver.

3. Hotel Pick-Up

  • For Hotel Pick-Up, please provide the complete hotel or villa name and address.
  • Please be ready at the confirmed pick-up time to help the tour run smoothly.

4. Weather & Safety

  • ATV dan rafting bergantung pada kondisi cuaca, kondisi jalur atau sungai, serta arahan instruktur dan pemandu.
  • For everyone’s safety, guests must follow the guide’s instructions throughout the activity.
  • If conditions are considered unsafe, the itinerary may be adjusted or the activity may be cancelled by the operator, with notice to guests.

5. Guest Fitness & Personal Items

  • Pastikan Anda nyaman mengikuti aktivitas ATV dan rafting serta beri tahu tim kami sebelumnya jika memiliki kebutuhan khusus yang dapat memengaruhi partisipasi.
  • Inform our team in advance of any relevant needs that may affect your participation.
  • Guests are responsible for their personal belongings. Please wear suitable footwear and bring any personal medication you may need.

6. Cancellations & Schedule Changes

  • Please contact AWAN TREKKING as soon as possible if you need to change your date or cancel your booking.
  • Any applicable cancellation, rescheduling, or refund arrangement should be confirmed with our team before booking.

7. Respect for Nature & Local Community

  • Jaga kebersihan jalur, sungai, dan lingkungan serta hormati masyarakat lokal selama aktivitas berlangsung.
  • AWAN TREKKING aims to provide a safe, respectful, and memorable experience for every guest.

What is included in the package?

The selected package combines either Single ATV or Tandem ATV with Ayung River Rafting. Please confirm the activity inclusions and schedule with our team when booking.

What is the difference between Single and Tandem ATV?

Single ATV is priced at IDR 1,100,000 per person. Tandem ATV is priced at IDR 1,600,000 per ATV for 2 people, and includes Ayung River Rafting.

Can you arrange hotel pick-up and drop-off?

Yes. Choose Hotel Transfer in the calculator and select your pickup area to see the round-trip transport fee per car.

How is the Tandem ATV price calculated for an odd number of guests?

Each Tandem ATV is for up to 2 people. The calculator rounds up the number of ATVs needed for your group.

How do I confirm my booking?

Submit your details through the booking form. WhatsApp will open with your selected package, date, participant count, transport option, and estimated total.
