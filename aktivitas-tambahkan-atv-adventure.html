BEFORE YOU RIDE

What to Prepare for Your ATV Adventure

Prepare for a comfortable ride and confirm the exact equipment and facilities with the ATV operator before your activity.

Comfortable Clothing

Wear clothes that can get dusty or muddy, and choose footwear suitable for an outdoor activity.

Safety Briefing

Listen carefully to the operator’s instructions and learn how to use the ATV before starting.

Operator-Provided Safety Gear

Confirm which safety equipment is provided and wear it as instructed by the operator.

Water & Personal Items

Ask whether drinking water is included and bring only essential personal items.

Pickup or Meeting Point

Confirm your pickup time, meeting point, and transport arrangement before departure.

Age & Riding Requirements

Check the operator’s age, height, health, and driving requirements before booking.

GET READY TO RIDE

What to Expect on Your ATV Adventure

Enjoy an outdoor ride through an ATV route selected by the operator. Route conditions, duration, and riding requirements can vary by provider and package.

  • Wear clothing that can get dusty or muddy and suitable footwear.
  • Follow the operator’s safety briefing and instructions throughout the ride.
  • Confirm the route, ride duration, age requirements, and any restrictions before booking.
  • Keep valuables secure and ask about storage facilities before the activity.
  • Confirm pickup or meeting-point details and what is included in your selected package.

ATV riding is an outdoor activity. Follow the operator’s guidance and do not ride beyond your ability.
