Our story

Rooted in Bali. Inspired by adventure.

AWAN TREKKING is a Bali-based trekking and tour service led by a local Mount Batur guide. We love sharing the island’s volcanic landscapes, beautiful viewpoints, and outdoor adventures with visitors from around the world.

Whether you want to hike before sunrise, explore black-lava terrain by Jeep, or combine your trip with another activity, we aim to make planning straightforward and the experience feel personal. We can help you choose an experience that fits your travel plans and group.
