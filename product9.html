    08.00 – 10.30

    Sesi Pagi: Penjemputan & Persiapan

    Fasilitas Antar-Jemput: Sopir ramah kami akan menjemput Anda tepat waktu di lobi hotel.

    Welcome & Safety Briefing: Setibanya di basecamp, nikmati secangkir kopi khas Bali. Instruktur profesional akan memberikan demo singkat cara mengoperasikan ATV (semi-otomatis atau transmisi manual), yang mudah dipelajari bahkan bagi pemula.

    Perlengkapan Keamanan: Setiap peserta wajib mengenakan helm standar keselamatan dan sepatu bot tahan air yang disediakan.

  2. 02

    10.30 – 12.30

    Sesi Petualangan Utama: Trek ATV 2 Jam

    Nikmati lintasan sepanjang kurang lebih 7–9 kilometer yang dirancang untuk memacu adrenalin sekaligus memanjakan mata.

    Track Lumpur & Hutan Tropis: Tantang diri Anda melintasi jalur berlumpur alami di bawah rindangnya pepohonan tropis.

    Terowongan Gelap (Cave Tunnel): Lewati terowongan buatan bawah tanah yang unik dan estetik untuk spot foto keren.

    Lembah Sungai & Air Terjun Mini: Berkendara di tepi sungai berarus jernih dengan cipratan air yang menyegarkan.

    Sawah & Perkampungan Lokal: Sapa warga lokal dan nikmati panorama terasering sawah hijau khas pedesaan Ubud.

  3. 03

    12.30 – 13.30

    Sesi Relaksasi: Makan Siang & Istirahat

    Fasilitas Bilas: Ruang ganti dan kamar mandi bersih dilengkapi sabun, handuk, dan air hangat untuk menyegarkan tubuh setelah bermain lumpur.

    Lunch Buffet: Nikmati makan siang prasmanan dengan menu kuliner Nusantara di restoran terbuka tepi lembah.

  4. 04

    13.30 – 16.00

    Penutupan & Kembali ke Hotel

    Gunakan waktu bebas untuk melihat hasil foto profesional yang diabadikan oleh tim fotografer di titik-titik terbaik selama perjalanan. Setelah itu, Anda akan diantar kembali ke hotel tempat menginap.

WHAT’S INCLUDED & EXCLUDED

Periksa fasilitas yang sudah termasuk dan biaya yang menjadi tanggungan pribadi sebelum memulai petualangan Anda.

  • Transportasi antar-jemput mobil AC privat (driver & BBM)
  • Briefing keselamatan dari tim operator ATV
  • Akses jalur ATV sesuai paket yang dipilih
  • Fasilitas dan minuman sesuai ketentuan operator
  • ATV dan perlengkapan keselamatan sesuai paket operator
  • Pengeluaran pribadi (snack tambahan, souvenir)
  • Pakaian yang nyaman dan siap terkena lumpur atau cipratan air
  • Sepatu tertutup dengan sol yang mencengkeram
  • Aktivitas tambahan yang tidak tercantum pada paket ATV yang dipilih
  • Tip untuk pemandu dan pengemudi
  • Sepatu olahraga dengan sol yang mencengkeram — gunakan alas kaki yang nyaman dan tidak licin saat berkendara ATV.
  • Pakaian nyaman — gunakan pakaian yang siap terkena debu, lumpur, atau cipratan air.
  • Kamera atau ponsel — untuk mengabadikan momen sunset dan pemandangan.
  • Tabir surya (sunscreen) — untuk membantu melindungi kulit dari paparan sinar matahari.
  • Tas kecil — untuk membawa barang-barang pribadi.
  • Obat pribadi — jika ada obat yang biasa Anda gunakan.
  • Pakaian ganti — disarankan setelah berkendara di jalur berlumpur atau basah.

Saran: Ikuti instruksi operator mengenai perlengkapan keselamatan. Konfirmasikan fasilitas yang termasuk dalam paket sebelum aktivitas.

1. Booking & Confirmation

  • A booking is confirmed once you receive confirmation from the AWAN TREKKING team.
  • Please provide accurate details, including your activity date, number of riders, ATV type, and hotel pick-up information.
  • Pick-up details and the driver’s contact information will be shared via WhatsApp before the activity.

2. Payment & Additional Charges

  • Cash payment is commonly accepted; please confirm the payment arrangement with our team when booking.
  • The price depends on the selected package, pick-up area, number of participants, and any optional activities selected.
  • Any additional charges will be explained before the activity.

3. Hotel Pick-Up & Drop-Off

  • Please provide the complete hotel or villa name and pick-up address.
  • Pick-up time and driver details will be confirmed via WhatsApp before the activity.
  • Please be ready at the confirmed pick-up time to help the trip run smoothly.

4. Weather & Safety

  • The ATV ride is subject to weather, route conditions, and instructions from the local operator.
  • For everyone’s safety, guests must follow the guide’s instructions throughout the activity.
  • If conditions are considered unsafe, the itinerary may be adjusted or the activity may be cancelled by the operator, with notice to guests.

5. Guest Fitness & Personal Items

  • Please follow the operator’s safety instructions and inform the team of any concerns before riding. ATV routes may include mud, water, and uneven terrain.
  • Inform our team in advance of any relevant needs that may affect your participation.
  • Guests are responsible for their personal belongings. Please wear suitable footwear and bring any personal medication you may need.

6. Cancellations & Schedule Changes

  • Please contact AWAN TREKKING as soon as possible if you need to change your date or cancel your booking.
  • Any applicable cancellation, rescheduling, or refund arrangement should be confirmed with our team before booking.

7. Respect for Nature & Local Community

  • Please keep the ATV route clean and respect the local community, other visitors, and the natural environment.
  • AWAN TREKKING aims to provide a safe, respectful, and memorable ATV experience for every guest.

How long does the Bali Quad Bike ATV Adventure berlangsung?

The duration depends on the selected ATV route and operator schedule. Please confirm the expected duration with our team before booking.

Is the ATV adventure suitable for beginners?

Beginner suitability depends on the operator’s route and safety requirements. All riders must attend the safety briefing and follow the operator’s instructions.

Do you provide hotel pick-up and drop-off?

Yes. Hotel pick-up and drop-off are available. The round-trip transfer fee depends on your accommodation area and will be shown in the calculator; some distant areas may require confirmation by our team.

What time will I be picked up?

Pick-up time depends on your location. Our team will confirm the schedule and share the driver’s contact details via WhatsApp before the activity.

Are ATV equipment and safety gear included?

Included equipment and safety gear depend on the ATV operator and selected package. Please confirm the inclusions with our team before booking.

What should I bring?

Wear comfortable clothes and closed shoes with good grip. Bring a change of clothes, sunscreen, and a secure waterproof pouch for your phone if needed. Confirm safety equipment and other inclusions before the ride.

Can I add another activity after the ATV ride?

Optional activities may be available depending on the package. Select the activity you want during booking and check the displayed additional costs before confirming.

Bagaimana cara memesan ATV?

Pilih jumlah Single dan Tandem ATV, tanggal aktivitas, serta area penjemputan hotel. Kirim detail Anda melalui WhatsApp dan tim kami akan mengonfirmasi pemesanan.

How do I pay?

Cash payment is commonly used. Please confirm the payment arrangement with our team when your booking is discussed.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Safety comes first. The ATV operator may adjust or postpone the ride if weather or route conditions are unsafe. Please follow the team’s guidance and contact us if you have questions before your activity.
