PLAN YOUR VISIT

What to Expect

Waterfall visits can include steps, uneven paths, wet rocks and changing weather. The exact route and facilities vary by waterfall.

  • Comfortable footwear: Wear shoes or sandals with good grip for potentially slippery paths.
  • Bring a change of clothes: You may get wet from spray or choose to enter the water where permitted.
  • Protect your belongings: Use a waterproof bag for your phone and valuables.
  • Follow local guidance: Respect signs, access rules and your guide’s safety instructions.

Waterfall visits are outdoor activities. Follow local guidance and your guide’s safety instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to help you plan a waterfall combination.

Can I add a waterfall to any tour?

Availability depends on the tour, route, timing and operator. Open the relevant package page to check the available activities.

Are entrance tickets included?

Inclusions can differ between packages. Please check the selected tour’s booking calculator and package details before confirming.

What should I bring?

Bring comfortable footwear, a change of clothes, drinking water, sun protection and a waterproof bag for valuables.

Can the itinerary change because of weather?

Yes. Weather, access conditions and local safety guidance may affect the route or timing.

How do I confirm the final price?

Select your preferred tour and review its calculator. Final cost may depend on group size, pickup area and chosen activities.
