Privacy Policy

Learn how AWAN TREKKING may use information shared during tour enquiries and booking coordination.

Website information · Please contact us if you need clarification before booking.
Important: This policy explains how information shared with AWAN TREKKING may be used when you contact us or enquire about a tour. Please do not send payment-card details, passwords, or other sensitive information through WhatsApp.

1. Information We May Receive

When you contact us or enquire about a tour, you may choose to provide information such as your name, WhatsApp or telephone number, travel date, hotel or pickup location, number of guests, selected tour, and details relevant to arranging your booking.

We only describe here information that may be shared with us through your enquiry or booking conversation. The information you provide will depend on the details needed for your particular tour.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use information you provide to:

  • Respond to enquiries and discuss tour options;
  • Confirm booking details, pickup arrangements, and payment instructions directly with you;
  • Coordinate the tour or related services you request;
  • Contact you about changes or practical details concerning your enquiry or booking; and
  • Respond to questions or resolve issues related to the service.

We do not state that every enquiry results in a booking, and a booking is not confirmed until the details have been agreed directly with AWAN TREKKING.

3. WhatsApp and Other Third-Party Services

Booking conversations may take place through WhatsApp. When you use WhatsApp, your use of that service is also subject to the provider’s own terms and privacy practices. AWAN TREKKING does not control how an independent third-party platform processes information under its own policies.

If a tour involves another service provider, we will discuss the relevant arrangements with you. Information needed to deliver a service may be shared with the relevant provider where appropriate to that arrangement.

4. Data Security and Retention

We take a practical approach to handling booking information, but no method of online communication or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please share only information relevant to your enquiry or booking.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to manage enquiries, bookings, operational records, or follow-up matters. A specific retention period has not been set out on this page.

5. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask what booking information you have provided, request a correction, or ask us to delete information where appropriate. We may need to retain some records where they are still needed to address a booking or other legitimate operational requirements.

6. Children’s Information

If you arrange a tour for a child, please provide only the information needed to make the booking and coordinate the experience. A parent or responsible adult should handle the booking and communications for children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This page may be updated as our booking process or services change. The version published on this website is the version visitors should review before sharing information.

8. Contact

For questions or requests concerning this Privacy Policy, contact AWAN TREKKING through WhatsApp at +62 851-1124-4457 or Instagram @awantrekking.
