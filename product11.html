Mengapa Mendaki Gunung Agung?

Gunung Agung adalah gunung tertinggi di Bali dengan ketinggian 3.142 mdpl. Pendakian privat ini menantang, namun membalasnya dengan sunrise di atas awan, lanskap vulkanik, dan panorama luas ke seluruh Bali. Cocok untuk pendaki yang bugar dan berpengalaman.

Sunrise di Atas Awan

Menyaksikan matahari terbit dari ketinggian, dengan lanskap vulkanik dan pemandangan luas ke seluruh Bali.

Dua Pilihan Rute

  • Pick-up and drop-off are normally at the same hotel. Contact us if you need a different drop-off location or pick-up outside our standard service areas.

4. Weather, Ceremonies & Safety

  • Mount Agung trekking may temporarily close during important Balinese religious ceremonies.
  • Rain, strong winds, clouds, volcanic activity, or unsafe trail conditions may affect availability. Our guides always prioritise safety when deciding whether the trek can continue.
  • Guests must follow the guide’s instructions throughout the activity.

5. Guest Fitness & Personal Items

  • Mount Agung is considerably harder than Mount Batur: expect steep climbs, rocky volcanic terrain, long hiking hours, and cold temperatures.
  • Good fitness and previous hiking experience are strongly recommended. The trek is generally not recommended for first-time hikers.
  • Guests are responsible for their personal belongings. Please wear suitable hiking footwear and bring any personal medication you may need.

6. Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • Free cancellation and free rescheduling when requested at least 24 hours before the scheduled start time.
  • A 50% cancellation fee applies if you cancel less than 24 hours before the start time.
  • A 100% cancellation fee applies if you cancel on the day of the tour or after the scheduled start time.
  • If severe weather makes it unsafe to start, you may receive a full refund or free rescheduling, depending on the guide’s and authorities’ recommendation. A weather forecast alone does not qualify for free cancellation.
  • Any bank or payment-processing fees related to a refund may be deducted according to your bank or payment provider’s policy.

7. Respect for Nature & Local Community

  • Please keep Mount Agung clean and respect the local community, Balinese customs, other visitors, and the natural environment.
  • AWAN TREKKING aims to provide a safe, respectful, and memorable experience for every guest.

How difficult is Mount Agung trekking?

It is challenging to very challenging. The trail has steep sections, rocky terrain, long hiking hours, and significant elevation gain. Good physical fitness and previous hiking experience are recommended.

How long does Mount Agung trekking take?

The Besakih Route takes approximately 10–13 hours round trip, and the Pasar Agung Route approximately 8–10 hours. Actual times vary with weather, trail conditions, and your pace.

Can beginners climb Mount Agung?

It is generally not recommended for first-time hikers. If you have little or no hiking experience, Mount Batur is usually a better choice because it is shorter and less demanding.

Which route is better, Besakih or Pasar Agung?

It depends on your fitness and goals. Besakih is longer and more challenging and reaches the true summit at 3,142 m. Pasar Agung is shorter but still demanding and reaches about 2,907 m.

What time does the trek start?

Besakih starts around 23.15 WITA and Pasar Agung around 02.00 WITA, so you can be near the top for sunrise. Hotel pick-up is arranged earlier depending on your area.

Do you provide hotel pick-up and drop-off?

Yes. Choose Hotel Transfer when booking; round-trip private transport is included in the price from standard pickup areas. Nusa Dua, Jimbaran, Uluwatu (+IDR 150,000 per group) and Amed, Tulamben (+IDR 200,000 per group) have a small surcharge.

Are breakfast, drinking water, and equipment included?

Yes. Breakfast, drinking water, a headlamp, a walking stick, and trekking entrance fees are included. Personal expenses, extra meals, personal gear, and travel insurance are not.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear hiking shoes, comfortable trekking clothes, and long pants. Bring a warm jacket, rain jacket, small backpack, personal medication, snacks, extra water, and a camera or phone.

How cold is it at the summit?

It can feel very cold before sunrise, especially with strong winds. Exact temperatures vary with weather and elevation, so warm clothing is strongly recommended.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Safety comes first. The itinerary may be adjusted, postponed, or cancelled according to local safety recommendations and your guide’s assessment. See the Terms & Conditions for refund and rescheduling details.

Is this a private tour?

Yes. Your trek is arranged as a private tour with your own group and private guide, with no shared trekking groups.
