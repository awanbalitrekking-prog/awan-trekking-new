A Tegallalang Rice Terrace Stop on Your Journey

Make your trip between Kintamani and Ubud even more memorable with an optional visit to the famous Tegallalang Rice Terraces. Discover Bali’s stunning agricultural landscapes, explore the walking paths, enjoy the peaceful surroundings, and capture unforgettable holiday moments along the way.

Your visit is entirely optional. After your tour is booked, AWAN TREKKING will check your travel route and schedule to see whether a suitable Tegallalang stop can be arranged. The exact timing will depend on your route, available time, and travel arrangements. There is no obligation to visit, walk the terraces, or purchase anything.

Please note: Entrance tickets, local conservation fees, swing rides, guide services, and other paid activities at the terrace may incur additional costs.

01

Before Your Activity

If the route and timing allow, you may visit Tegallalang before starting your trekking or other adventure. It can be a refreshing and picturesque start to your day.

02

After Your Activity

Prefer a relaxing break afterward? If it fits the return route and available time, you may stop at the rice terraces to take in the views and unwind.

03

Your Trip, Your Choice

Let AWAN TREKKING know if you would like to visit. We will check the route and available time; if you prefer not to stop, your trip can continue as planned.

A BALINESE RICE TERRACE EXPERIENCE

Discover the Beauty of Subak Landscapes

Set in the lush valley north of Ubud, the Tegallalang Rice Terraces offer a world-famous view of traditional Balinese cascading fields and palm-fringed hillsides. Activities vary by venue, exact location, and availability.

01 · SCENIC WALK

Scenic Ridge Walk & Exploration

  • Take a gentle walk along the ridges and pathways of the terrace, where available.
  • Admire the multi-layered green steps carved into the valley slope.
  • Enjoy panoramic views of local farms and towering coconut trees.

02 · LOCAL CULTURE

Learn About Subak Irrigation

  • Discover Bali’s traditional cooperative water management system, known as Subak, which has sustained local agriculture for centuries.
  • See how water flows from higher springs down through the terraced plots.
  • Gain insight into local farming traditions and community life.

03 · LOCAL REFRESHMENTS

Refreshment & Local Café Breaks

  • Many local spots and open-air cafés line the ridge, offering fresh coconut water, local snacks, and refreshing drinks overlooking the valley.
  • Enjoy a relaxing break with a direct view of the green landscape.

04 · PHOTO OPPORTUNITIES

Scenic Photo Spots & Swings

  • Enjoy breathtaking photo opportunities.
  • Depending on the exact location along the ridge, you may find scenic viewing decks, artistic photo installations, or popular Bali swing rides.

05 · LOCAL SHOPPING

Browse Local Handicrafts

  • Browse small stalls and shops run by local residents offering handmade wooden carvings, straw hats, woven bags, and traditional Balinese souvenirs.
  • All purchases are optional and paid for directly by guests.

WHY ADD THIS STOP?

A Refreshing Pause in Your Bali Journey

Tegallalang’s rice terraces offer a refreshing pause during your Bali journey, a chance to witness iconic island landscapes, and an opportunity to support local rural communities. A stop may fit after a Sunrise Jeep Tour, Mount Batur Trekking, or an ATV activity when the route and schedule allow.

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Tips for Visitors

A few simple tips to help you enjoy your rice terrace stop.

Best Time to Visit

  • Early morning offers cooler temperatures and soft lighting for photography.
  • Late afternoon is also wonderful for a relaxed stroll and sunset valley views.
  • Ask AWAN TREKKING whether the stop fits before or after your planned activity.

What to Bring

  • A camera or smartphone for photos.
  • Comfortable walking shoes with good grip, as pathways can be steep, narrow, or uneven.
  • Cash or another accepted payment method if you plan to buy refreshments, souvenirs, or optional experiences.

CLEAR & TRANSPARENT

Good to Know Before You Visit

We want every guest to know what the stop includes and which costs are separate.

If You Choose to Visit

  • AWAN TREKKING can arrange a complimentary stop at the Tegallalang area as part of the agreed journey, when the route and schedule allow.
  • Time to look around public viewing areas and accessible paths.
  • The freedom to explore without any obligation to purchase or participate in paid attractions.

What Guests Pay For

  • Refreshments, snacks, souvenirs, or other items they choose to buy.
  • Entrance tickets, conservation donations, parking, or local path fees required by the local village management.
  • Optional activities, swing rides, or guided services if chosen.

An Optional Stop — No Pressure, Just Possibility

A Tegallalang Rice Terrace visit is entirely your choice. If you would like to go, AWAN TREKKING can arrange the stop without an additional arrangement fee when it fits your agreed route and schedule. Any purchases, entrance fees, parking charges, or optional activities requested at the location are paid separately by guests. Availability and costs depend on the specific spot visited along the terrace.

You are welcome to visit, walk around, and enjoy the scenery without buying anything — or skip the stop and continue your adventure.
