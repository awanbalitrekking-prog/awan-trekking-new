Why Is the Mount Batur Jeep Tour the Best Way to Enjoy the Sunrise?

Enjoy the beauty of the sunrise from the Mount Batur area in a more comfortable and enjoyable way, without hiking for hours.

With a 4WD Jeep, you can take in stunning natural panoramas, from Mount Agung and Mount Abang to the charming Lake Batur. The trip gets even more exciting with an off-road adventure across the Black Lava fields, an exotic formation of black lava rock and a favorite photo spot.

PREPARE FOR YOUR ADVENTURE

WHAT TO BRING

Items to bring to enjoy the sunrise and the off-road trip comfortably.

Warm Clothing

A jacket or warm layers, as the air before sunrise can feel cold.

Comfortable Footwear

Closed shoes or comfortable footwear for walking on soil and volcanic rock.

Personal Items

Drinking water, tissues, personal medication if needed, and cash for extra expenses.

Camera or Phone

Bring a device to capture the sunrise and scenery, and make sure the battery is fully charged.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers to help you prepare for your Jeep sunrise adventure.

Does the Jeep Tour require hiking?

The Jeep Tour takes you to vehicle-accessible points along the route. Some locations may still require a short walk.

How many people fit in one Jeep?

Jeep packages are priced per vehicle, with a general capacity of 1–3 participants per Jeep. For larger groups, additional Jeeps may be needed.

Is hotel transfer included?

Transfer depends on the option you choose. With the meeting point, you come to the meeting point yourself; with hotel transfer, the fee and pickup area will be confirmed based on your location.

Which Trip should I choose?

Trip 1 focuses on the sunrise. Trips 2–5 add locations or experiences such as the Floating Temple, Black Lava, and Black Sand, according to the package name and details.

Can the itinerary change?

Yes. The order and timing of visits may adjust to weather, traffic, safety, and operational conditions on site.

When will I receive the driver's details?

Pickup details and the driver's contact will be confirmed by the AWAN TREKKING team before the activity.
