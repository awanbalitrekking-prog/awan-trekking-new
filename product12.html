KEUNGGULAN PENGALAMAN

Why Trek Mount Abang?

Mount Abang (2,152 m) is the highest point on the Batur caldera rim, northeast of Mount Batur in Bali. Its trail is quieter and slightly more challenging than Mount Batur, making it ideal for travelers seeking a more peaceful sunrise.

Sunrise from the Highest Point of the Batur Caldera

Reach the summit at around 5:30 AM, then enjoy breakfast while waiting for sunrise.

Wide Panoramic Views on Clear Days

From the summit, you may see Mount Rinjani on Lombok, Mount Agung, and Mount Batur and its lake to the west.

Quiet and Challenging Trail

The route passes through dense tropical forest followed by slippery slopes. Mount Abang is much less crowded than Mount Batur.

Optional Coffee Plantation Stop

After the trek, choose to stop at a coffee plantation or go straight back to your hotel.

Total Tour Duration

Approximately 8–9 hours

How difficult is Mount Abang?

It is challenging, and somewhat harder than the Mount Batur sunrise trek. The path crosses tropical forest, then a slippery uphill track.

How long does the tour take?

The trek starts around 02:30 and you reach the summit around 05:30. The tour ends around 09:00–09:30, about 8–9 hours including pick-up.

What will I see from the summit?

On clear days you can see Mount Rinjani, Mount Agung, and Mount Batur with its lake. Mount Abang often has rain and cloud, so views are not guaranteed.

Is this a private tour?

Yes. Only your group joins, with your own private guide.

Do you offer hotel pick-up?

Yes. Choose Hotel Transfer for a private round trip from your area, or Meeting Point for a lower price.

What is included?

Private guide, private car (Hotel Transfer), breakfast, snacks, drinking water, trekking equipment, a poncho, and all entrance tickets.

Is it suitable for first-time hikers?

Mount Abang is somewhat harder than Mount Batur, so Mount Batur is the gentler choice if you have little hiking experience.
