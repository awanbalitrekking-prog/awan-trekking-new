JEEP DAY TRIP ADVENTURE

Why Choose the Mount Batur Jeep Day Trip?

Explore the volcanic landscape of Mount Batur by 4WD Jeep, visit Black Lava and Black Sand, and add Floating Temple on the packages that include it.

Feel the thrill of off-roading across exotic black lava rock fields, set against outstanding scenery that is perfect for capturing your holiday moments.

WHAT’S INCLUDED & EXCLUDED

Check what is included in the Mount Batur Jeep Day Trip package and the extra costs you may need to prepare before you go.

INCLUDED IN THE PACKAGE

  • Professional 4WD Jeep: A 4x4 Jeep ride with an experienced local driver who can guide visitors to the best photo spots.
  • Hotel Pickup & Drop-off (Optional): A private air-conditioned vehicle with a driver for certain areas, such as Ubud, Kintamani, Sanur, Kuta, Seminyak, Canggu, and Denpasar. The transfer fee depends on the area and must be selected when booking.
  • Entrance Tickets & Levies: Mount Batur Geopark area tickets and village/Kintamani levies: what is covered in the package price must be confirmed before booking.
  • Jeep Route According to Your Package: Trip 1: Black Lava & Black Sand. Trip 2: Black Lava, Black Sand & Floating Temple. Additional entrance or destination access fees must be confirmed.
  • Mineral Water, Breakfast & Hot Drinks: Mineral water, breakfast, and hot drinks should not be assumed to be included in the base price; availability must be confirmed before booking.
  • Blanket or Warm Jacket: If needed, the availability of a blanket or warm jacket must be confirmed first and is not automatically included.
  • Natural Hot Spring (Optional): Not included in the base price; only available if chosen as an add-on and its price and availability have been confirmed.

NOT INCLUDED IN THE PACKAGE

  • Hotel Transfer: Not included in the base Jeep price; available as an optional extra with a fee based on the pickup area.
  • Hot Spring & Additional Activities: Tickets or fees for additional activities, including the hot spring, are not included unless selected and confirmed in writing.
  • Unconfirmed Destination Access Fees: Floating Temple tickets, certain destination tickets, parking, or levies not covered by the package must be confirmed before the activity.
  • Personal Expenses & Tips: Personal shopping, extra food or drinks, and tips for the driver are not included.
  • Breakfast & Warm Gear: The base package does not promise breakfast or a blanket/jacket. Availability must be confirmed first.

PREPARE FOR YOUR ADVENTURE

WHAT TO BRING

For the Mount Batur Jeep Day Trip, prepare practical gear to explore the Black Lava, Black Sand, and Floating Temple areas according to your chosen package. This is a daytime Jeep trip, not a sunrise trekking package.

Comfortable, Closed-Toe Shoes

Choose comfortable, non-slip shoes for walking on black sand or uneven volcanic surfaces.

Sun Protection

Bring a hat or sunglasses and use sunscreen, especially in sunny weather.

Camera or Smartphone

For taking photos of the Mount Batur landscape and the spots visited during the tour.

Drinking Water

Bring drinking water to stay hydrated during the trip and while walking around the destinations.

Small Backpack

Use a small bag to carry your drinking water, sunscreen, phone, and personal items.

Cash & Light Jacket (Optional)

Bring cash for personal needs or confirmed extra fees. A light jacket can be brought if you tend to feel cold.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers to help you prepare for your Mount Batur Jeep Day Trip.

What time does the Mount Batur Jeep Day Trip start?

The start time depends on your pickup location and selected package. Your confirmed pickup time will be shared before the activity.

How many people can fit in one Jeep?

One Jeep accommodates up to 3 people. Groups of more than 3 people will need additional Jeeps.

What is included in the Jeep Day Trip?

Inclusions depend on the trip you choose. Please check the Included in the Package section before booking.

Which Jeep Day Trip packages are available?

There are two packages: Trip 1 visits Black Lava and Black Sand; Trip 2 adds Floating Temple. Prices are Rp800,000 or Rp900,000 per Jeep, with a capacity of 1–3 people per Jeep.

Is sunrise included in this package?

No. Mount Batur Jeep Day Trip is a daytime tour and does not include a sunrise experience.

What should I bring?

For the Jeep Day Trip, bring comfortable closed-toe shoes, sun protection, a camera or smartphone, drinking water, and a small backpack. Cash for personal expenses may be useful; a light jacket is optional.

Can you arrange hotel pickup?

Hotel transfer may be selected when available. Choose Hotel Transfer and provide your pickup area when making your booking.

How can I book the tour?

Select your preferred trip and booking details, then use the Book Now option to send your request to AWAN TREKKING.
