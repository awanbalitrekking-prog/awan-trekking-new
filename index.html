FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers to plan your Mount Batur adventure with Awan Trekking.

How difficult is the Mount Batur trek?+

The sunrise trek involves walking uphill on volcanic terrain and is generally suited to people with a moderate level of fitness. Conditions can vary, so let us know if you have concerns before booking.

Do you provide hotel pickup?+

Hotel transfer can be arranged for selected tour packages and pickup areas. Choose hotel transfer when booking and share your accommodation location so we can confirm the details and price.

What should I bring?+

We recommend comfortable walking or trekking shoes, a warm layer, water, and a small bag. For sunrise trekking, bring a light jacket because it can be chilly before dawn.

Can I choose a private tour?+

Private options are available for selected experiences. Check the details of your chosen package or contact us to discuss the group size and the experience you prefer.

How do I book a tour?+

Explore the tour packages, select the experience that suits you, and use the booking option on its page. You can also contact Awan Trekking on WhatsApp to ask questions before confirming.

Can I add other activities to my trip?+

Some packages can be combined with activities such as ATV rides, rafting, waterfalls, hot springs, or scenic stops. Available options and prices depend on the selected package.

What is included in the tour price?+

Inclusions vary by experience. Please check the details on the individual tour page for what is included, what is optional, and any additional costs before booking.

How can I confirm pickup details?+

Share your preferred tour, date, group size, and accommodation or meeting point with us on WhatsApp. We can then confirm the available pickup arrangement and meeting details for your chosen package.

Still have a question? We’re happy to help you plan your trip.
